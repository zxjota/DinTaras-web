2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we handle for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account and to provide the features you request.
  • To operate, maintain, and secure the App.
  • To understand how the App is used so we can improve performance, fix defects, and develop new features.
  • To communicate with you, including responding to your requests and sending important notices about the App.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use information collected from the App to target you with advertising on third-party services unless we clearly disclose that use and obtain any consent required by law.

We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers. With vendors who process information on our behalf (for example, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, or customer support), bound by contractual obligations consistent with this policy.
  • Legal requirements. When we believe disclosure is necessary to comply with a valid legal process, applicable law, or a governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of DinTaras, our users, or others.

The App may use privacy-preserving analytics or crash reporting to help us understand usage and diagnose problems. We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the App, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed for these purposes, we delete it or anonymize it in accordance with our internal retention practices.
